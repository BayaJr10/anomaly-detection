Which columns are needed?

Required: LOCATION, NEIGHBORHOOD, PM25, PM10, and DATETIME or DATE + TIME (UTC). Optional: PM1, HUMIDITY, TEMPERATURE, NEIGHBORHOOD_LAT, NEIGHBORHOOD_LON.

Include the 72 hours before the period you care about, and all sensors for the same hours, so the history and neighbour checks are complete.

Alert threshold

–

A reading is flagged when the model’s anomaly probability reaches this value. Lower it to catch more, raise it to see only the clearest cases.

Severity rules

A flag is Critical or Alert if either test passes, otherwise Watch. PM2.5 bands follow the US EPA 2024 breakpoints.

Unusualness is the robust z-score of the excess over the usual level.
